## Fire Stick / Android TV

Requires Android 7.0 or later on Android TV or an Android-based Fire TV device. Install the TV APK for browsing, library management, account profiles, compatible add-ons, and playback with your remote. Playback support depends on your device and media source.

## Android

Requires Android 7.0 or later. Install the Android APK for the phone and tablet interface, including browsing, library, account settings, compatible add-ons, and playback.

## iPhone

Requires iOS 16.1 or later. Download the IPA and sign it with your Apple account through a sideloading tool. The iPhone app is offered as a beta; feature support may vary by device and configured source.

## Catalogs & add-ons

On TV 0.3.18 and Android 0.4.17, open **Settings → Add-ons** for four separate destinations: **Current Add-ons**, **Discover Add-ons**, **Sync Add-ons** and **Script Providers**. Browse **Omnia Official** and **Community** sources; already-installed HTTP add-ons stay in Current Add-ons and leave the available list. Home catalog choices and Collections are together under **Settings → Content**.

The current iPhone 0.4.10 Beta uses **Settings → Content & Discovery → Catalogs & add-ons → Community Add-ons**. Its next settings and safe-sync update is pending. Older clients can read saved add-ons, but editing an account upgraded to safe sync requires a compatible client.

Select a category, open a provider's setup page, and install its configured manifest when required.

Catalogs, HTTP add-ons, collection layouts and script-provider repositories use different installation formats. Follow each entry's setup instructions.

## Restore your preferences

On the latest TV and Android apps, open **Settings → Restore default settings**. This entry is available in Essentials and Advanced. Confirm the reset to restore preferences while keeping your account, installed add-ons, Collections, library and watch history.

## Updating

On Android and TV, use the app's update checker or install the matching APK over your existing installation. Do not uninstall first. The app closes while the system applies an update.

On iOS, use your sideloading tool to sign and install updates. APKs do not work on iPhone.

Choose your platform above rather than relying on GitHub's repository-wide Latest badge. Media sources and provider subscriptions are separate from Omnia.
