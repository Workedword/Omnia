# Choose your Omnia app

[Back to downloads](README.md)

| Platform | Package | Interface |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire Stick / Android TV | TV APK | Remote and directional navigation |
| Android phones & tablets | Android APK | Touch navigation |
| iPhone | iOS IPA · Beta | Touch navigation |

## Fire Stick / Android TV

Install the TV APK for browsing, library management, account profiles, compatible add-ons, and playback with your remote. Playback support depends on your device and media source.

## Android

Install the Android APK for the phone and tablet interface, including browsing, library, account settings, compatible add-ons, and playback.

## iPhone

Requires iOS 16.1 or later. Download the IPA and sign it with your Apple account through a sideloading tool. This is a beta; physical-device installation and account sync are not yet verified.

## Updating

On Android and TV, use the app's update checker or install the matching APK over your existing installation. Do not uninstall first. The app closes while the system applies an update.

On iOS, use your sideloading tool to sign and install updates. APKs do not work on iPhone.

Choose your platform above rather than relying on GitHub's repository-wide Latest badge. Media sources and provider subscriptions are separate from Omnia.
