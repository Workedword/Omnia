## Fire Stick / Android TV

Requires Android 7.0 or later on Android TV or an Android-based Fire TV device. Install the TV APK for browsing, library management, account profiles, compatible add-ons, and playback with your remote. Playback support depends on your device and media source.

### Try the TV beta

[Omnia TV 0.3.32 Beta](https://github.com/Workedword/Omnia/releases/tag/v0.3.32%2Btv) offers Streams, Omnia TV and OmniaTube as optional sections.

**Omnia TV** supports portal setup with a persistent virtual MAC, playlist URLs/files and Xtream accounts. Give the displayed virtual MAC to your provider, save the portal, then choose Connect after activation. Use Categories, Channels, Guide, Favourites and Recent to browse your sources. XMLTV programme information is available when supplied.

**OmniaTube** has video shelves for Home, Live, Music, Gaming, News and Sports, with channel and playlist browsing. Its keyboard opens only when you choose Search. Google account sign-in is not included.

The 0.3.32 beta adds portal Movies and Series with provider categories, paged posters, seasons and episodes where supplied. Use Previous, Next or Channels from live playback, and open **Guide** in TV Settings for IPTV setup and troubleshooting. Large portal channel lists load without keeping the full provider response in memory.

Select **Settings → About → Update channel → Beta** for in-app test updates. Portal compatibility depends on your provider, and Tube playback needs normal-device feedback. The full visual redesign is ongoing, and compact poster labels can be clipped. Xtream currently supports live TV. Catch-up, a timeline guide, portal-supplied programme data, Tube account/library features and live add-on routing remain in development. These sections are TV-only for now; the phone and iOS packages keep their current interface. Stable TV updates remain on 0.3.27.

## Android

Requires Android 7.0 or later. Install the Android APK for the phone and tablet interface, including browsing, library, account settings, compatible add-ons, and playback.

Android 0.4.25 adds Home catalog groups and layout preview, Watched History in Library, a setup Guide, and an Essentials/Advanced settings choice. Advanced is selected by default and keeps the existing applicable controls.

## iPhone

Enjoy Omnia’s mobile interface on iPhone with iOS 16.1 or later. Download the IPA, then sign and install it with your Apple account through a sideloading tool. The current iOS 0.4.25 beta includes Home catalog groups and layout preview, Watched History, Guide, and the mobile Essentials/Advanced settings choice. This is a beta; feature support and performance can vary. Apple silicon Mac compatibility is experimental, and the download remains an iPhone IPA.

## Your account

Create an Omnia account with your email and a password, then use that account on each device. If you forgot your password—or previously used a social sign-in without setting one—choose **Forgot password** and follow the email instructions. Your existing profiles, library and history remain attached to your account. After signing in, you can optionally link Discord to manage that same account through the bot.

## Catalogs & add-ons

On TV 0.3.27, Android 0.4.25 and iOS 0.4.25 Beta, open **Settings → Add-ons** for four separate destinations: **Current Add-ons**, **Discover Add-ons**, **Sync Add-ons** and **Script Providers**. Browse **Omnia Official** and **Community** sources; already-installed HTTP add-ons stay in Current Add-ons and leave the available list. Home catalog choices and Collections are together under **Settings → Content**.

iOS 0.4.25 Beta uses the same four Add-ons destinations and Content layout. Compatible-settings sync is optional: leave it off for device-specific preferences. Sharing settings requires compatible app versions on the participating devices.

Select a category, open a provider's setup page, and install its configured manifest when required.

Catalogs, HTTP add-ons, collection layouts and script-provider repositories use different installation formats. Follow each entry's setup instructions.

In **Choose Home Catalogs**, group rows by add-on and use **Show all / Hide all** for each group. New rows are marked **New** without changing your existing order. Preview before **Apply**, or cancel to keep your layout. Catalog presentation choices share only when settings sync is enabled.

## Restore your preferences

On the latest TV, Android and iOS beta apps, open **Settings → Restore default settings**. Confirm the reset to restore preferences while keeping your account, installed add-ons, Collections, library and watch history.

## Updating

On Android and TV, use the app's update checker or install the matching APK over your existing installation. Do not uninstall first. The app closes while the system applies an update. On compatible older Android and Fire OS versions, **Reopen after update** is an optional setting. Other devices use **Open** in the system installer.

On iOS, use your sideloading tool to sign and install updates. APKs do not work on iPhone.

Choose your platform above rather than relying on GitHub's repository-wide Latest badge. Media sources and provider subscriptions are separate from Omnia.
